Why Neck & Shoulder Pain Is Complex

The neck and shoulder region is one of the most structurally complex areas of the body. The cervical spine, shoulder girdle, and upper thoracic region all interact — meaning a problem in one area creates compensation patterns across all three. This is why standard treatments often only provide temporary relief.

  • Chronic neck stiffness and limited rotation
  • Shoulder impingement and restricted lifting
  • Radiating arm pain or hand tingling
  • Tension headaches from neck tightness
  • Upper back muscle knots and trigger points
