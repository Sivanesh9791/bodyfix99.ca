Understanding Post-Concussion Syndrome

A concussion is not just a brain injury — it affects the entire cranial and cervical structural system. When the head experiences trauma, the cranial bones, meningeal membranes, and upper cervical vertebrae absorb the impact. Without proper structural correction, these areas remain in a state of strain — causing prolonged symptoms that can last months or years.

  • Persistent headaches after head trauma
  • Brain fog and difficulty concentrating
  • Dizziness and balance problems
  • Light and sound sensitivity
  • Sleep disturbances and fatigue
  • Neck pain following the injury
