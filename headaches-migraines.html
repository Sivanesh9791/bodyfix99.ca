Why Most Headache Treatments Don't Last

Most headache treatments focus on managing symptoms — pain medication, rest, cold compresses. But chronic headaches and migraines have a structural component that is rarely addressed. Suboccipital muscle tension, upper cervical nerve entrapment, and cranial suture strain are common physical drivers of recurring headaches that osteopathic care can directly target.

  • Tension headaches from neck and suboccipital tightness
  • Cervicogenic headaches originating from the upper spine
  • Cluster headaches with cranial nerve involvement
  • Migraines with postural and structural triggers
  • Post-concussion headaches
