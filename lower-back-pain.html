Understanding Chronic Lower Back Pain

Lower back pain is one of the most common and debilitating conditions affecting people in Fort McMurray. Long hours of physical labor, sitting in heavy equipment, or repetitive movements cause cumulative structural damage that builds up over years — until one day the body simply can't compensate anymore.

  • Lumbar facet joint compression
  • Deep muscular guarding and spasm
  • Sacroiliac joint dysfunction
  • Disc pressure and vertebral misalignment
  • Postural imbalances from repetitive work
